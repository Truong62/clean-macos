More than a cleaner

The everyday tools a developer's Mac needs, in one calm, native window.

Jira

Jira that feels like Notion

Your sprint as a clean tree of tasks, subtasks and bugs. Filter by assignee, status, app or month — without the slow web UI.

  • Table and Board views, nested subtasks
  • Notifications when you're assigned or mentioned
  • Lives in the menu bar too
Ports

What's on port 3000? Now you know

Every port your processes are listening on, with the command that started it and how long it has been running.

  • Localhost vs. network exposure at a glance
  • Open in browser, Quit or Force Quit
  • Only your own processes — nothing to break
Scheduled jobs

Cron job failing? See the log

LaunchAgents and crontab entries in one list, with a human-readable schedule, last exit status and the tail of their log.

  • Run now, turn off or delete a job
  • Failed jobs stand out in red
  • System agents shown read-only
Power recovery

Power cut at night? Your Mac picks itself back up

One flow shows every step that brings an unattended Mac back: power on, log in, open your apps, start background jobs and lock the screen.

  • Turn on when power returns, log in without a password
  • Your login items and background jobs, toggled in place
  • One-click jobs like keep the screen on or mute at login
  • Lock the screen a few seconds after it logs in
  • Asks for your password only when a setting needs it

Install

Up and running in a minute

Download

Grab the latest .dmg and drag Clean macOS into Applications.

Open it once

The app is free and not notarized, so right-click it → Open the first time, or run:

xattr -cr /Applications/CleanMacOS.app

Grant access

Turn on Full Disk Access so the app can measure protected folders. It detects the change by itself.

After an update macOS may ask for access again — turn the switch off and on. That's the price of a free app without a paid Apple certificate.
